Questions & Answers

What's included in the free audit?

It depends on where you are. If you don't sell yet, we check whether your product will be in demand on Amazon. If you already sell, we analyze your listing and show how to increase sales. If you have a Shopify store, our developer reviews the site and advises how to increase sales.

Which platforms do you work with?

Amazon, TikTok Shop and Shopify, plus advertising in Meta (Facebook, Instagram) and Google Ads. Our team also has hands-on experience with AliExpress, Ozon, Wildberries, Yandex Market and other marketplaces.

I don't have a product yet. Can you help?

Yes. We research niches and competitors, select a product for your budget and goals, build a financial model, find and verify a supplier — and launch the brand on Amazon.

Can you register a US company for selling on Amazon?

Yes. We pick the best state (Florida, Delaware, etc.), register an LLC or Corporation, get an EIN, prepare the formation documents and Operating Agreement, and set up a business address. The company is ready for Amazon, Walmart, Shopify, Payoneer and US banks.

Do you work with factories in China?

Yes. We run a tender among manufacturers, shortlist the top 5, check certificates (CE, FDA, ISO, etc.), negotiate price, MOQ and packaging, and organize logistics to Amazon FBA or a 3PL.

Can you take on one task instead of the full cycle?

Yes. We can run the full marketing cycle or handle a specific task — PPC, listings and content, a Shopify store, TikTok Shop, or ads in Meta and Google.
